# Controlled pilot and commercial readiness boundary

Phase 9 completes SkillWard’s planned application roadmap for controlled fictional QA and preparation for an authorised pilot. Software completion is not the same as permission to process real workforce or clinical material.

Before a real-customer pilot, the operator and customer must approve the pilot scope, roles, local content, privacy handling, security risk, incident contacts, retention, support, backup/recovery objectives, accessibility approach and success/exit criteria. Contracts, insurance and applicable Australian legal or regulatory advice remain owner responsibilities.

Before unrestricted commercial launch, add production support and monitoring ownership, tested backup restoration, vulnerability and penetration testing, approved privacy/terms/data-processing documents, subprocessors, breach response, billing/refunds, service levels and customer onboarding/offboarding procedures. Provider-dependent email, push, SSO, provisioning and external reporting remain disabled until configured and accepted.

The installable PWA is a web application. Apple App Store or Google Play distribution requires a separate native packaging, signing, privacy declaration, account and store-review program.
