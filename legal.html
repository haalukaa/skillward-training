Privacy Policy

SkillWard is designed to support workforce training, progress tracking and competency management. Depending on how an organisation configures the platform, information may include a staff member’s name, employee identifier, work email, role, department, assigned training, progress, assessment results and competency status.

How information may be used

  • Provide and administer training access.
  • Track learning progress, deadlines, assessments and competency sign-offs.
  • Support authorised trainers and managers in workforce development and compliance activities.
  • Maintain platform security, reliability and audit records.

Access and retention

Access should be limited according to workplace role and organisational authority. Participating organisations are responsible for applying their own retention, privacy and record-management obligations. Users should not enter patient information or unnecessary sensitive personal information into SkillWard.

Questions and corrections

Contact your organisation’s SkillWard administrator to request access to, correction of or information about records associated with your account.

Terms of Use

By accessing SkillWard, users agree to use the platform only for authorised workplace training and competency activities, protect their login details, provide accurate information and follow their organisation’s policies.

Users must not attempt to access another person’s account, bypass security controls, disrupt the platform, upload harmful material, or copy and distribute protected training content without permission.

SkillWard training content supports workplace learning. It does not replace workplace policies, clinical guidelines, mandatory instructions, professional judgement or advice from an authorised supervisor. If content conflicts with an organisation’s current policy, users must follow the organisation’s approved policy and report the discrepancy.

Access may be restricted or suspended when required for security, compliance, employment-status or administrative reasons. Platform features and content may be updated as training requirements evolve.

Accessibility

SkillWard aims to provide an inclusive experience across desktop and mobile devices, including keyboard navigation, readable contrast, clear labels and compatibility with common assistive technologies.

If you experience an accessibility barrier, contact your SkillWard administrator and include the page, task, device and assistive technology involved. This information helps the issue be assessed and corrected.

Contact & Support

For account access, training assignments, deadlines, competency records or technical problems, contact your organisation’s SkillWard administrator or management team.

When reporting a problem, include what you were trying to do, what happened, the device and browser used, and a screenshot when appropriate. Do not include patient information or passwords.
